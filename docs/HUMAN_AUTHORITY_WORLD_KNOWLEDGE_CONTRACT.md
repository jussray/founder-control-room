# Human Authority and World Knowledge Contract

## Purpose

Founder Control Room and every governed Juss project must treat authorized humans as the root of consequential authority and treat the world as a continuing source of observations, experiences, evidence, and knowledge candidates.

Systems may reason, recommend, simulate, observe, retrieve, compose, and execute within granted authority. They may not manufacture authority from knowledge, confidence, provider capability, past approvals, model output, automation, or evidence alone.

## Root authority

Human authority comes first.

Canonical order:

```text
Authorized human
→ intent
→ bounded authority / approval
→ project-owned control plane
→ capability / provider / tool
→ execution
→ evidence
→ outcome
→ new experience
```

- AI, models, agents, MCPs, plugins, providers, workflows, automations, learned knowledge, prior receipts, and sibling projects never create or renew human authority.
- Knowledge can inform a proposal. Evidence can prove or disprove a claim. Neither authorizes a consequential mutation by itself.
- Human authority does not alter reality. An authorized human may decide what to do next, but cannot make failed execution become truthful success.
- Approval is scoped to the exact current subject, action, consequence, and authority boundary required by the applicable repository or product contract.
- If the subject, scope, head, provider, runtime, consequence, or required evidence moves, reacquire current authority when the applicable contract requires it.

## The world as knowledge and experience

Treat legitimate real-world interaction as evidence-bearing experience, including user reports, customer responses, runtime behavior, provider responses, deployments, experiments, analytics, repository history, documents, market observations, operational failures, and verified outcomes.

Canonical learning loop:

```text
World
→ observation
→ provenance
→ evidence classification
→ experience receipt
→ knowledge candidate
→ contradiction / red-team check
→ context + confidence + freshness
→ working knowledge
→ decision or proposal
→ authorized action when applicable
→ outcome evidence
→ new experience
→ update / supersede knowledge
```

Experience is not automatically truth. One observation must not silently become a universal rule.

Working knowledge must preserve, where applicable:

- subject and scope;
- source and provenance;
- observation time / evidence window;
- evidence classification;
- confidence or uncertainty;
- applicability context;
- contradiction history;
- freshness / invalidation conditions;
- predecessor and successor fingerprints or proof cookies; and
- the evidence that caused promotion, challenge, supersession, or retirement.

## Knowledge states

Use a lifecycle appropriate to the product, such as:

```text
OBSERVED
→ INTERPRETED
→ HYPOTHESIS
→ TESTED
→ SUPPORTED
→ DURABLE
→ CHALLENGED
→ SUPERSEDED
```

A repository may use stricter or more domain-specific states. It may not collapse observation, inference, authority, execution, and outcome into one state.

## Independence on Self

Every governed project must preserve its core identity, contracts, data semantics, authority model, evidence meaning, and recovery path when an external AI, model, API, SDK, connector, runtime, vendor, or sibling project disappears or materially changes.

Connection may multiply capability. Disconnection must not erase identity, truth, authority, critical state, or recoverability.

For each critical dependency classify:

```text
REPLACEABLE
DEGRADED_FALLBACK
HARD_DEPENDENCY
UNKNOWN
```

A hard dependency must be explicit, founder-visible, and paired with the best available data-exit, rollback, recovery, or migration path. Do not claim independence where none has been proved.

## Separation rules

Keep these questions independent:

- **Knowledge:** What do we currently believe, and why?
- **Experience:** What happened or was observed?
- **Authority:** Who is allowed to decide or authorize what?
- **Evidence:** What proves the claim?
- **Execution:** What action did a system or human perform?
- **Outcome:** What actually became true?

No plane may silently stand in for another.

## Human-first safety and user agency

Human authority is bounded by applicable safety, privacy, legal, age, consent, product-isolation, and repository rules. A system must not interpret "human authority first" as permission to bypass those boundaries.

For user-facing products, especially products involving children or teens, experience learning must not become covert surveillance, manipulation, profiling, or a substitute for consent. Collect and retain only information that is legitimate for the product purpose and current authority.

## Portfolio inheritance

This contract applies to Founder Control Room and every repository governed by the Founder Intelligence inheritance registry. Repository-local rules may specialize or strengthen it. They may not weaken:

- human-root authority;
- separation of authority from truth;
- provenance and evidence requirements;
- knowledge freshness and supersession;
- provider / sibling-project independence;
- recovery and data-exit expectations;
- privacy, safety, consent, and consequence boundaries; or
- the rule that evidence and knowledge never self-authorize mutation.

## Required audit questions

For every material project, capability, provider, model, connector, or sibling-project dependency, answer:

1. If this dependency disappeared now, what stops, what survives, and what evidence proves it?
2. Can the project still preserve identity, critical state, authority, evidence, and recovery?
3. What real-world observations are being learned from, and what provenance do they retain?
4. Which conclusions are observations, hypotheses, supported knowledge, or superseded knowledge?
5. Who currently holds authority for the proposed consequential action?
6. Could any provider, model, learned rule, stale approval, or automation accidentally create or widen authority?
7. What contradiction or failure would make the current knowledge fingerprint stale?
8. What rollback, degraded mode, manual recovery, or data-exit path exists?

## Challenge stack

Apply the existing founder workflow:

```text
Reality
→ ULTRATHINK
→ Red Team 1 — premise
→ Lindy mode
→ L99
→ OODA
→ smallest reversible implementation
→ focused proof
→ Red Team 2 / provider-loss + knowledge-corruption attack
→ Documentation Truth
→ Rollback
→ Next Gate
```

During Reality and Red Team 2, always ask:

> If the provider, model, sibling project, or current learned assumption vanished or proved false now, what would survive, what would fail, who retains authority, and what evidence proves the answer?

## Truth boundary

Documentation is architecture evidence only. It does not prove runtime survival, failover, knowledge correctness, human approval, execution, deployment, provider behavior, data portability, or human outcome.

Prove those layers separately.
