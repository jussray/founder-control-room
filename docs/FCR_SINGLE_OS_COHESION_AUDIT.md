# Founder Control Room Single-OS Cohesion Audit

## Purpose

Founder Control Room is the single founder operating system and product shell.

Chief AI Machine, PromptOS, StoryEngine-backed capabilities, L99, Proof Mode, OODA, goalfix, agents, skills, providers, automations, analytics, and future add-ons may exist as implementation layers or named capabilities, but they must not become competing operating systems for the founder.

The founder should express intent once. FCR must coordinate the capabilities needed to advance that intent.

## Canonical founder-intent loop

```text
Founder Intent
→ Reality / Current State
→ Capability Selection
→ Authority
→ Action
→ Evidence
→ Outcome
→ Next Gate
```

Every FCR-internal subsystem must plug into this loop rather than inventing a parallel one.

## Cohesion invariants

A material FCR or cross-repository audit must verify all of the following:

1. **One intent authority**
   - The founder goal is canonical.
   - A subsystem may refine, decompose, or route the goal but may not silently replace it with its own product goal.

2. **One founder-facing operating model**
   - Internal implementation names must not force the founder to choose among competing operating systems.
   - Founder-facing surfaces should expose capabilities and outcomes, not an agent or framework zoo.

3. **Shared state, explicit ownership**
   - Each fact has one authoritative source.
   - Subsystems may cache or interpret state but may not create a conflicting truth plane without an explicit reconciliation contract.

4. **Shared authority contract**
   - No agent, workflow, prompt, provider, repo, or subsystem may grant itself authority.
   - Consequential authority remains bounded by FCR governance and explicit founder approval.

5. **Shared evidence contract**
   - Code, test, CI, deployment, runtime, provider execution, analytics, and human outcome remain separate proof layers.
   - Evidence generated by one subsystem must be consumable by the common FCR proof model rather than trapped in subsystem-local success language.

6. **Shared next-gate contract**
   - Every capability returns the founder to one understandable next decision or action.
   - A subsystem may recommend a next step but may not fork the founder into a separate lifecycle that FCR cannot observe.

7. **Composable future additions**
   - A future capability must declare its input intent, required state, authority ceiling, action contract, evidence output, rollback, and next-gate behavior before becoming an FCR capability.
   - New functionality should extend FCR rather than create another OS beside it.

8. **Consequence-only infrastructure attention**
   - Provider change noise becomes founder work only when it can materially change security, reliability, cost, limits, runtime behavior, integration behavior, deployment viability, or a dated migration/deprecation obligation for the active stack.
   - Prioritize official releases and deprecations, security or reliability incidents, limits/pricing/runtime changes, and important integration changes. Routine changelog noise is not a founder task.
   - Bind every surfaced infrastructure claim to current provider evidence and the affected product path. A provider incident is provider-state evidence, not proof of an application defect.
   - Collapse each infrastructure review to the one or two highest-value founder gates. Do not create six parallel vendor backlogs when no product action is required.

## Infrastructure consequence filter

For Cloudflare, Supabase, Firebase, Expo, n8n, Vercel, and future infrastructure providers, classify a newly observed provider change before routing it into founder work:

```text
MATERIAL
= requires migration, security response, reliability mitigation, cost/limit review,
  runtime adjustment, integration change, or evidence-model correction for an active product path

WATCH
= credible provider change with plausible future relevance but no current action gate

NOISE
= routine release/changelog activity with no material effect on an active founder goal

UNKNOWN
= impact cannot yet be tied to current provider evidence or a known product path
```

A `MATERIAL` classification must return:

1. what changed and the authoritative provider evidence;
2. which FCR-managed product path is affected;
3. why the effect is consequential now;
4. the smallest reversible review or repair;
5. at most two founder review gates unless additional action becomes necessary as evidence changes.

Do not convert `WATCH`, `NOISE`, or `UNKNOWN` into implementation work merely because a vendor announced something new.

## Architectural drift test

Treat a change as **FCR cohesion drift** when it introduces any of these without an explicit compatibility reason and reconciliation path:

- a second canonical goal or mission state;
- a second founder approval system;
- a second incompatible evidence or outcome model;
- a second founder-facing dashboard that acts as an independent control plane;
- duplicated orchestration that can issue conflicting actions;
- subsystem-local completion claims that FCR cannot verify;
- a new `OS`, `control room`, `command center`, or equivalent abstraction that competes with FCR rather than extending it;
- routing that requires the founder to understand repository, agent, framework, or provider boundaries before expressing the outcome they want.

When drift is detected, prefer the smallest reversible repair that reconnects the subsystem to the shared founder-intent loop. Do not solve cohesion problems by deleting useful specialized capability.

## Project audit questions

For every FCR-related project or subsystem under review, answer:

```text
INTENT
What founder outcome does this capability advance?

STATE
Which source owns the facts it consumes and produces?

CAPABILITY
What specialized work does it perform for FCR?

AUTHORITY
What may it do, and what still requires founder/FCR approval?

ACTION
What mutation or recommendation can it produce?

EVIDENCE
What exact proof does it return to FCR?

OUTCOME
How is founder-goal progress distinguished from mere execution success?

NEXT GATE
What one decision/action returns to the founder?

INFRA CONSEQUENCE
Is any provider change MATERIAL, WATCH, NOISE, or UNKNOWN for this exact product path?

COHESION
Does this extend FCR, or accidentally create a parallel operating system?
```

Any `COHESION` answer other than **extends FCR** is a blocker for expansion until reconciled.

## Current subsystem interpretation

The intended relationship is:

```text
Founder Control Room
= single founder OS, product shell, shared state/governance/evidence/approval/outcome plane

Chief AI Machine
= reasoning, synthesis, capability composition, routing, and executive recommendation layer

PromptOS
= prompt, skill, instruction, and agent-behavior governance layer

StoryEngine-backed capability
= story/content creation and provenance capability when invoked through FCR

L99 / OODA / Lindy / Red Team / goalfix / Proof Mode
= internal methods and verification disciplines

Providers / plugins / n8n / GitHub / Cloudflare / Supabase / Shopify / other tools
= bounded execution or evidence surfaces
```

These labels describe responsibilities. They do not create separate founder-facing operating systems or independent authority.

## Product boundary

Not every Juss-owned product must become FCR.

A separate end-user product may retain its own product experience, data boundary, runtime, and safety model. Se'kret Bip, for example, must preserve its heightened teen/family privacy and safety boundaries.

When a separate product is observed or operated through FCR, the integration must still obey the FCR founder-intent, authority, evidence, and next-gate contract. FCR coordination does not erase product isolation or grant cross-product data access.

## Audit disposition

Classify each reviewed component as one of:

- `COHESIVE` — extends the common FCR founder-intent loop without duplicating control authority;
- `COMPATIBLE_WITH_BOUNDARY` — separate product/system boundary is intentional and its FCR integration is explicit;
- `DRIFT` — competing goal/state/authority/evidence/user-control model exists;
- `UNKNOWN` — evidence is insufficient to classify.

Do not call a project cohesive based on naming, documentation, or architecture diagrams alone. Code/runtime behavior and real-path evidence remain separate verification layers.

## Rollback

This document changes governance and audit expectations only. It does not itself migrate schemas, route traffic, merge repositories, rename products, or move data.

If this contract proves too broad, revert the governance commit and preserve the prior project boundaries while a narrower contract is reviewed. Never "fix" cohesion by collapsing privacy, security, billing, deployment, or product-data boundaries.
