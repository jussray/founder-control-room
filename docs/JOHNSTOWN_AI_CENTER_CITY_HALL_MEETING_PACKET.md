# Johnstown AI Center — City Hall Working Meeting Packet

Status: current internal working-meeting packet for the September 23 commercial-site plan

This packet is designed for one City Hall working session. It does not imply City endorsement, sponsorship, pilot approval, financing approval, site approval, property approval, or any other external commitment.

## Meeting purpose

Turn the current Johnstown AI Center plan into a defined City-side decision path for a 90-day proof-first launch tied to an eligible commercial Johnstown site-control path and a property-inclusive financing structure.

The current plan no longer uses the earlier $25,000 partner/rented-space-only Stage 1 model as the active financing frame. That earlier plan remains historical provenance only.

## The 90-day launch in one paragraph

Johnstown AI Center is proposed as a practical AI workforce, small-business implementation, and entrepreneurship center serving Greater Johnstown and Cambria County. The first 90 days are still the proof window, but the operating plan is now anchored to a dedicated eligible commercial site or another documented commercial site-control structure acceptable to the applicable financing path. The capital plan therefore includes site acquisition or lease/site control, renovation, equipment, software, insurance and professional setup, working capital, workforce, participant delivery, and operating reserve. A purchase path may be pursued, but this packet does not claim that City rules require ownership rather than another acceptable form of commercial site control.

## Who the 90-day launch is for

- Local small businesses that need practical AI diagnostics, implementation, training, or ongoing support.
- Residents who want practical AI and digital-workforce skills.
- Aspiring founders and participant teams moving from an idea or workflow problem to a testable project or paid outcome.

## Current measurable targets

The September 23 operating targets are:

1. 10 participating businesses.
2. 10–20 participant projects.
3. 6 delivered AI-enabled workflows.

Attendance, account creation, workflow execution, and visibility do not count as paid demand by themselves. Paid conversion, retained use, repeat purchase, and funded employer or partner demand stay separate.

## Commercial-site model

The current operating plan is property-inclusive rather than partner-space-only.

Acceptable paths to evaluate with the City and lenders include:

- purchase of an eligible commercial property;
- a signed commercial lease or other site-control instrument that satisfies the applicable program; or
- another documented commercial site-control structure the City and lender confirm is eligible.

The project should not represent ownership as a City requirement unless the City states that requirement in writing. It should, however, treat an eligible commercial site and documented site control as a load-bearing financing/readiness requirement.

Any specific property remains a candidate until current availability, commercial eligibility, zoning/use fit, building condition, acquisition terms, renovation scope, insurance, utilities, and site-control evidence are independently verified.

## Property-inclusive capitalization frame

The old $25,000 Stage 1 ask is superseded for the current property-inclusive plan.

The current working capitalization envelope is:

- planning range: **$552,390–$736,390**;

This is a planning model, not an approved loan amount or a representation that every recipient should fund the full project.

The capitalization must cover the complete uses-of-funds stack rather than only portable equipment:

- commercial acquisition, lease, or other site-control costs;
- renovation / code / fit-out work;
- devices, networking, AV, and operating equipment;
- software, cloud, cybersecurity, and business systems;
- insurance, accounting, legal, permits, and professional setup;
- workforce and payroll burden;
- participant and program delivery;
- marketing and customer acquisition where eligible;
- working capital and operating reserve; and
- contingency appropriate to the verified property and renovation scope.

A prior working model carried a **$245,000 renovation allowance** and modeled one commercial-property candidate at **$139,900**. Those figures remain planning inputs only until the property, scope, quotes, and site-control path are re-verified. The ten-part-time-role decision also requires the workforce line to be recalculated against actual weekly hours and payroll burden before the capitalization target is treated as final.

## Current financing posture

Before a formal City Job Development Loan or other property-related financing application is treated as ready, the project still needs to reconcile the final package with current program requirements, including:

- an eligible private non-residential City location and acceptable site-control evidence;
- the applicable City financing-share cap and resulting total eligible project cost;
- required owner equity;
- remaining private or other financing commitments;
- treatment of ten part-time $15/hour roles under applicable job-creation rules;
- current acquisition/lease, renovation, equipment, software, insurance, working-capital, and operating quotes; and

If verified property facts, quotes, payroll assumptions, or underwriting requirements materially change the use-of-funds assumptions, the financing model must be revised before a formal application. The project should not inflate or shrink the request merely to fit a program ceiling.

## Explicit City Hall asks for this working session

The meeting should end with answers, owners, or written next steps for these six asks:

1. **City-side owner:** Who, if anyone, owns the next City decision or coordination step for this initiative?
2. **Commercial-site rule:** What exact property/site-control forms are acceptable, and what location, zoning/use, building-condition, or documentation requirements must be satisfied?
3. **Financing structure:** What City financing component, financing-share cap, owner-equity level, job-creation treatment, and outside-financing evidence apply to the current property-inclusive capitalization model?
4. **Workforce treatment:** How will ten part-time roles at $15/hour be treated for job-creation or workforce-program eligibility, and what wage/hour documentation is required?
5. **Pilot evidence:** Which of the current 90-day targets or additional evidence would City economic-development staff want to see before a larger commitment or subsequent phase?
6. **Next submission:** What exact document, application, site-control proof, quote, partner proof, lender proof, or committee step should happen next, who receives it, and on what timetable?

## Stop / rollback conditions

The first 90 days remain evidence-led even though the capital frame is larger:

- If an intended property fails eligibility, inspection, zoning/use, financing, or site-control review, move to another eligible commercial site rather than forcing that property.
- If acquisition is not financeable but an eligible commercial lease/site-control structure is, preserve the center plan without falsely claiming ownership is required.
- If verified renovation or operating quotes push the project outside the supportable capitalization range, re-scope the site or financing stack before signing irreversible commitments.
- If the ten-part-time workforce line cannot be supported by funded demand, workforce programs, or working capital, stage actual hours/start dates without changing the $15/hour target or pretending unfunded hires exist.
- If the 90-day business, workflow, retention, paid-outcome, or repeat targets materially miss, stop expansion and diagnose demand before compounding fixed costs.
- If City participation remains review-only, continue building lender, JARI, workforce, redevelopment, employer, and strategic-partner evidence without representing City sponsorship that does not exist.
