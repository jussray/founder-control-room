# Founder Signal Engine — Zapier 100-Task Budget

Status: `LOCKED_SCOPE`

Authoritative budget: [`config/zapier-task-budget.json`](../../config/zapier-task-budget.json)

Verification: `npm run verify:zapier-budget`

## Reality

Ray reports 126 monthly Zapier tasks in the current scope while the active Zapier free plan includes 100 monthly tasks.

A scoped outcome is not automatically a billable task. Zapier counts successful billable action executions. Triggers and supported built-in control steps such as Filters, Formatter, Paths, Delay, Looping, Sub-Zaps, Digests, Zapier Manager, Storage, Tables, and Forms are assigned to the free control plane in this contract.

The repository does not contain a current Zapier History export proving which live actions produced the reported 126. Therefore:

- `126` is the founder-reported baseline;
- `88` is the locked planned monthly budget;
- the reduction is a scoped architecture target, not proof that the live Zapier account has already changed;
- no existing Zap may be deleted or disabled automatically.

## Verified Buffer constraint

Buffer can prepare and schedule content across many social networks, but posts ultimately belong to individual connected channels.

The current Zapier Buffer `Add to Queue` action requires one target `Channel`. Buffer's current GraphQL `createPost` mutation also requires one `channelId`; Buffer documents that multiple-channel publishing requires one post mutation per channel.

Therefore this budget does not pretend that three social channels cost one Zapier task:

```text
one approved campaign
x three selected Buffer channels
= three Buffer action tasks
```

Buffer's current Free plan supports up to three connected channels at one time. The runtime must inspect the live Buffer connections rather than hard-code LinkedIn as the only channel. If Ray's Buffer plan exposes more channels, the Zapier budget gate still controls how many can be automated in a given campaign.

## Goal

Preserve the required Founder Signal Engine, FutureYou, ME, social, investor-outreach, failure, and evidence outcomes while staying below the Zapier free-plan limit:

```text
100 monthly task limit
- 10 emergency reserve
= 90 operating ceiling
- 88 planned tasks
= 2 routine headroom
```

The 88-task plan reduces the founder-reported baseline by 38 tasks, or about 30%, while expanding automated social distribution from one channel to three channels per approved campaign.

## Consolidated system

### Free intake and routing plane

Normalize every source event into one Zapier Table queue:

```text
GitHub, HubSpot, Gmail, Buffer, or approved manual request
-> source trigger
-> free Filter and Formatter steps
-> free Zapier Table queue record
```

Each queue record must include:

```text
source_event_id
signal_id
source
source_record_url
associated_deal_id
mode
publish_allowed
requested_outcomes
requested_social_channels
idempotency_key
created_at
```

Do not call AI, Buffer, Gmail, or another paid external write before the budget and idempotency gates pass.

### One core analysis lane

The queue feeds one core Founder Signal processor:

```text
new queued signal
-> free duplicate and monthly-budget gates
-> one structured AI analysis action
-> free validation and routing paths
-> one same-deal HubSpot result note
-> free proof and budget records in Zapier Tables
```

One AI action must generate the complete machine-mappable result, including:

```text
5W1H
VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN
ME present-founder decision lens
FutureYou five-year decision lens
Chief AI routing decision
missing evidence
recommended next action
social campaign angle
social media brief
platform-native review drafts
investor-outreach draft when requested
publish_allowed: false
```

Platform-native review drafts must cover:

```text
LinkedIn
Facebook
Instagram
Threads
X
TikTok
YouTube Shorts
Pinterest
Bluesky
Mastodon
Google Business
```

Do not spend separate AI tasks for FutureYou, ME, each social channel, investor drafting, or proof formatting.

### Parallel Buffer approval lane

Social content remains review-only until an exact founder approval event exists.

```text
approved social campaign
-> free approval, idempotency, and budget reservation gates
-> choose three unique active Buffer channels
-> free Paths or Looping fan-out
-> one Buffer draft or queue action per selected channel
-> free returned-artifact ledger records
```

Rules:

1. Do not default to LinkedIn only.
2. Select three unique live Buffer channels using founder approval, audience fit, platform readiness, media compatibility, queue capacity, and recent coverage.
3. Use the platform-specific copy generated by the shared AI response. Do not paste identical generic copy everywhere.
4. Reserve all three projected tasks before starting the fan-out so the campaign does not cross the operating ceiling midway through execution.
5. Capture each Buffer item ID, channel, status, and timestamp separately.
6. Keep non-selected platform variants as review drafts in HubSpot and Founder Control Room.
7. Rotate channel selection over time when that improves reach, while preserving the founder-approved campaign intent.
8. At the budget ceiling, create no Buffer actions. Keep the complete campaign package in the free backlog for manual Buffer composition or the next task month.

A typical first trio may be LinkedIn, a Facebook Page, and an Instagram Business or Creator account, but only live Buffer connection evidence may establish the actual trio.

### Investor outreach lane

Investor research and draft preparation happen inside the core AI response. The paid action is reserved for the real approved send:

```text
approved personalized investor email
-> free approval and budget gates
-> one email send action
-> free evidence ledger record
```

Do not batch unrelated investors into one impersonal message merely to save tasks. Do not send without exact founder approval for the recipient and content.

### Failure lane

Use the Zapier-supported error trigger for genuine failures:

```text
Zap error
-> free Zap-name and correlation filters
-> one same-deal HubSpot failure task
-> free failure ledger record
```

Do not create routine success tasks in addition to the canonical HubSpot result note. A HubSpot task is reserved for a real failure or an explicit founder follow-up.

For partial Buffer fan-out failure, record which channels succeeded before retrying. Never replay the whole campaign blindly and create duplicate posts.

### Founder Control Room proof

The same-deal HubSpot result note, Buffer returned artifacts, and Zapier Table records are the canonical Zapier-produced proof artifacts.

Founder Control Room must read or ingest those artifacts through its own runtime instead of requiring a second Zapier write for every event. This preserves Control Room evidence while avoiding a duplicated paid action.

## What was merged conceptually

1. FutureYou, ME, Chief AI, 5W1H, evidence, investor copy, media direction, and all platform drafts become one structured AI response per signal.
2. HubSpot result-note creation becomes the single routine external proof write.
3. Routine HubSpot success tasks are removed; tasks are failure-only or founder-requested follow-ups.
4. Buffer fans out in parallel to three founder-approved active channels rather than remaining LinkedIn-only.
5. Non-selected channels keep platform-native review drafts without spending more AI tasks.
6. Founder Control Room reads from the canonical ledger instead of receiving a duplicate Zapier write for every signal.
7. Source normalization, counters, idempotency, routing, backlog, and digests use free Zapier control-plane steps.

## Budget gate

Before every billable action or social fan-out:

1. Read the current month from the free budget table.
2. Confirm the idempotency key has not already completed the requested action.
3. Calculate the full projected task count, including every selected Buffer channel.
4. Reserve the projected count before fan-out.
5. Continue only when the projected count is at or below `90`.
6. At `91+`, write the request to the free backlog table and stop before the billable action.
7. The final 10 tasks remain emergency reserve and require an explicit founder-approved override.

## Risk

- A live Zap may contain hidden premium or billable actions not represented in repository docs.
- AI, Code, SDK, MCP, and app actions may consume more than the simple one-action estimate depending on the configured product and runtime.
- Buffer connections, channel permissions, media requirements, and posting modes differ by network.
- A three-channel campaign can partially succeed. Idempotency must be channel-specific.
- Polling volume and incoming trigger volume can cause platform limits even when Filters later stop billable actions.
- The task reduction is not active until the real Zapier and Buffer workflows are inspected and repaired through connected control surfaces.

## Rollback

Disable only the new consolidated Zaps and restore the prior named Zaps. Preserve Zap History, HubSpot notes/tasks, Buffer drafts/posts, Zapier Table rows, and Founder Control Room evidence. Do not delete prior workflows or records.

## Stop condition

Stop new routine billable work at the 90-task operating ceiling. Queue overflow for review or the next billing month. Do not silently exceed the free plan, broaden publication authority, duplicate posts, or delete existing automation to manufacture a lower count.
