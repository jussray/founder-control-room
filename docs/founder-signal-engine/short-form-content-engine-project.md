# Founder Short-Form Content Engine

## Goal

Turn verified GitHub and product milestones into proof-first, wonder-rich short-form media packages for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, LinkedIn, and later channels without flattening the idea into literal proof cards.

Accuracy is necessary but not sufficient. The media must earn attention, create curiosity or feeling, preserve comprehension and human agency, and make proof part of the scene rather than using the receipt itself as the whole scene.

## Canonical visual contract

All founder-content stills, moving clips, covers, carousels, and product-derived launch visuals must pass `tools/founder-content-contracts/visual-wonder-contract.cjs`.

The contract has two stages:

```text
thesis + human outcome
→ emotional intent
→ native form
→ visual hook
→ scene / metaphor
→ proof object + truth boundary
→ generation / production
→ Attack 2000 artifact gate
→ founder review
```

`Attack 2000` is a two-pass falsification pressure budget. It is not a claim that 2,000 external tests or actions ran.

Pass 1 attacks the concept before generation: premise, literalism, visual curiosity gap, emotional pull, proof fit, human outcome, native format, brand/canon fit.

Pass 2 attacks the rendered artifact: proof integrity, nonliteral interpretation, accessibility, canon integrity, human agency, scroll-stop power, beauty, wonder, meaning, platform nativeness, memorability, brand fit, clutter, safe zones, reduced motion where applicable, legibility, AI-slop tells, and visual proof overclaim.

A truthful but dull artifact fails. A beautiful but misleading artifact fails. A visually impressive artifact that merely restates the caption fails the nonliteral gate.

## Channel growth layer — integrated, not a second OS

The channel-growth mechanics live inside this existing Founder Short-Form Content Engine and its existing YouTube growth evaluator. They do not create a separate creator OS, publication authority surface, analytics subsystem, or StoryEngine replacement.

The durable sequence is:

```text
audience + painful problem + useful promise
→ repeatable format
→ content engine
→ return/community loop
→ money path
→ measure
→ compound / repair / kill
```

Every channel or recurring series should declare an `audience/problem/promise` tuple before production. Do not guess a niche from vibes alone. Use observed audience questions, search intent, current conversations, product evidence, creator/community feedback, and platform-native analytics where authorized.

A repeatable format is a production and recognition contract, not permission to make repetitive content. The same format may recur while the underlying proof, story, example, viewer problem, emotional job, and visual treatment change.

### Four content jobs

Every piece should have one primary job and may have one secondary job:

- `DISCOVER` — attract new viewers through a real problem, search intent, experiment, lesson, or surprising finding;
- `PROVE` — show receipts: product behavior, GitHub/runtime evidence, demonstrations, before/after outcomes, failures, fixes, and inspectable proof;
- `BELONG` — create return behavior through recurring series, shared language, questions, polls, comments, traditions, milestones, and community conversation;
- `CONVERT` — give a qualified viewer a legitimate next action toward an offer, product, service, resource, community, email relationship, or other measured money path.

Do not treat every post as a conversion post. Do not let `CONVERT` disappear from the channel architecture merely because a specific post is discovery or community content.

### Canonical content unit

The default unit is:

```text
HOOK → VALUE → PROOF → PAYOFF → CTA
```

`VALUE` is the useful idea, answer, demonstration, or transformation. `PROOF` shows why the claim deserves belief. `PAYOFF` makes the significance legible to the viewer. `CTA` is one next action, not a pile of asks.

For founder/product content, proof should normally be visible inside the story rather than becoming a detached receipt slide.

### Return loop

The engine optimizes for remembered usefulness and return behavior, not only one-off views.

Recurring series, follow-up questions, polls, comment prompts, viewer-requested continuations, milestones, and community responses are learning signals. They do not grant publication authority and they do not prove traction by themselves.

A successful content pattern is not promoted because it looked good or received one spike. Promotion requires subject-matched outcome evidence against the lane's intended North Star and, for repeatability claims, more than one confirmed run receipt.

Failed or inconclusive patterns are retained as revision memory rather than deleted. Preserve what missed, why it may have missed, what was changed, and what the next experiment is intended to falsify.

### Faceless format guardrails

Faceless is a format choice, not a quality shortcut.

Allowed:

- original voiceover with purposeful B-roll;
- tutorials and screen demonstrations;
- narrated slides or data stories when motion and information design are intentional;
- product proof, founder-build footage, animation, character storytelling, and cinematic metaphors;
- combinations of human voice, product UI, evidence, generated visuals, and licensed/original assets.

Rejected:

- copied clips with no original value;
- repetitive or reused content with only cosmetic changes;
- generic AI slideshow spam;
- copyrighted footage without appropriate rights;
- misleading thumbnails or claims;
- zero-original-value compilations;
- automation that removes the point of view, evidence, or useful transformation.

A face is optional. A point of view, original value, and accountable truth boundary are not.

### Money path

Monetization is designed early but earned through value. Platform ad revenue is a downstream bonus path, not the only business model.

The preferred stack is tested from closest legitimate dollar outward:

1. services or implementation help tied to a proven viewer problem;
2. owned products, apps, tools, templates, courses, or digital resources where they fit;
3. memberships or community offers where recurring value exists;
4. affiliate offers only when relevant and properly disclosed;
5. sponsorships with brand/audience fit;
6. platform revenue when current platform eligibility is independently verified.

Do not hard-code platform monetization thresholds into product truth. Eligibility rules change. Current thresholds must come from current provider/platform evidence when they are decision-relevant.

`views`, `subscribers`, `likes`, `comments`, and `watch time` are not revenue. An affiliate link or sponsor intention is not revenue. Revenue requires outcome evidence such as a verified sale, collected payment, attributable payout, or other destination-native financial receipt.

### Growth control loop

The existing authenticated route `/automation/conveyor/founder-content/youtube-growth/evaluate` remains the YouTube advisory control plane.

It now exposes the channel system contract alongside the existing evidence-gated 90-day evaluator:

```text
audience/problem/promise
→ repeatable format
→ LEEVIZE
→ separate publication approval
→ measure
→ return loop
→ diagnose
→ money path
→ double-down / repair / kill
```

Calendar time only decides when a phase may be considered. It never proves a winner. `TEST_AND_VALIDATE → DOUBLE_DOWN → SCALE` still requires fresh sourced measurements, explicit criteria, repeatability receipts, continuity, and the existing non-authorizing evaluation contract.

The evaluator remains advisory-only. It cannot publish, schedule, spend, scale execution, merge, deploy, or convert a target into outcome evidence.

### System responsibilities

Keep one operating chain rather than spawning adjacent systems:

```text
Chief / Council
→ choose and attack the content bet

StoryEngine
→ narrative, script, beat, series, and story structure

LEEVIZE
→ visual canon, shot jobs, motion, continuity, proof footage, captions, and production direction

Founder Content lifecycle
→ draft, review, approval, scheduling/publishing boundary, provider readback, receipts

FCR growth evaluator
→ measurement, diagnosis, repeatability, money-path truth, compound/repair/kill decision support

PromptOS / reusable libraries
→ preserve prompt lineage and promote only outcome-proven patterns; unsuccessful patterns stay in revision memory
```

No subsystem above may silently mint founder approval, publication authority, provider credentials, revenue claims, or proof that belongs to another plane.

A merge, connected account, generated draft, scheduler configuration, beautiful render, or platform upload is not proof of publication, runtime equivalence, or traction.

## Creative modes

Use one primary mode rather than defaulting every proof story to a dashboard:

- `cinematic-proof` — real evidence staged inside a compelling visual world;
- `mythic-founder` — worldview, ambition, identity, or founder thesis expressed through symbolic imagery;
- `dream-product` — product possibility shown as an emotionally legible future moment;
- `character-story` — canon-preserving character narrative;
- `product-experience` — actual product experience where interaction/motion carries meaning.

The proof object is a prop or anchor inside the story. It should not automatically become the entire visual composition.

## Short-form motion grammar

For 6–15 second social-native clips, default to:

```text
0–1s   scroll-stopping visual event
1–4s   thesis lands
4–8s   proof object or product truth enters
8–12s  meaning twist / human significance
12–15s memory line / loop-close
```

The timing may change when the idea demands it, but a moving clip must have an intentional motion language. Static slides exported as video do not satisfy the contract merely because the file format is MP4.

## /human visual gate

Before approval, answer:

- What does this leave the human understanding that they did not understand before?
- What feeling opens the door to that understanding without manipulating them?
- Does the visual preserve choice, context, dignity, and truth?
- Is the piece rewarding to encounter even before the viewer reads the full caption?
- Does beauty clarify meaning, or is it hiding weak substance?

A technically correct artifact that is visually dead is incomplete. A beautiful artifact that reduces comprehension or overstates proof is rejected.

## Key and bridge rule

Use the existing protected key reference `zapier-founder-signal-engine`. Do not create a duplicate key unless the founder separately authorizes rotation or replacement.

The key is the credential path. The Founder Signal Engine bridge is the callable path. The matching Zapier run is execution proof. Raw secrets must never appear in GitHub, chat, HubSpot, Buffer, Canva, screenshots, logs, or evidence artifacts.

## Existing-key health test

The first safe test is authentication-only and non-mutating:

```bash
bash scripts/test-openai-key-health.sh
```

The script reads `OPENAI_API_KEY` from the environment, calls the OpenAI Models endpoint, prints only HTTP status and a redacted result, and never prints the key. A successful authentication test proves only that the key is accepted by OpenAI. It does not prove Zapier, Buffer, HubSpot, social publishing, or the Founder Signal Engine bridge executed.

The project is not complete until one real invocation can be linked to:

- reachable deployed Founder Signal Engine bridge;
- invocation receipt;
- matching workflow/provider run ID when that adapter is used;
- verified OpenAI output;
- approved voice and visual assets;
- Canva or equivalent render artifact when used;
- platform-specific review artifacts;
- Visual Wonder / Attack 2000 verdict;

- separately authorized HubSpot evidence;

Visual approval, source proof, provider execution, publication, and audience outcome remain separate evidence layers.
