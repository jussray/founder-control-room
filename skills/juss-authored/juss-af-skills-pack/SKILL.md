# JUSS AF SKILLS PACK

## Kernel boundary

This pack is a subordinate creative-production module under `juss-os`.
Before running it, load `../juss-os/SKILL.md` and `../juss-os/references/os.md`.
This pack may specialize creative execution, but it cannot override `juss-os` authority, phase, evidence, state, receipt, merge/deploy, or verification gates.

## Mission

Turn founder intent into original, high-conviction advertising and visual storytelling that:
- fits the actual product,
- preserves continuity,
- proves claims,
- drives a measurable next action,
- learns from outcomes,
- and compounds into reusable Juss-owned capability.

This pack is not a wrapper around Arcads or any other outside system.

External systems may be inspected for useful ideas, but no external pattern enters production until it passes the Juss adoption gate.

# 2. CORE PRODUCTION SEQUENCE

Canonical sequence:

**INTENT → AUDIENCE → TENSION → CRITERIA → OPTIONS → PROOF → DECISION → ACTION → OUTCOME → LEARNING**

This is the default spine, not a rigid template.

## INTENT
Capture what the founder actually wants the audience to:
- understand
- feel
- believe
- do

Intent may come from text or voice.

## AUDIENCE
Define:
- who this is for
- what they already know
- what they care about
- what makes them hesitate
- what outcome matters to them

## TENSION
Show the real friction before the product appears.

No invented pain points.

## CRITERIA
Make the decision logic visible.

Examples:
- budget
- speed
- safety
- ease
- quality
- family fit
- reliability
- compatibility
- trust
- time saved

## OPTIONS
Show meaningful alternatives when comparison helps the buyer decide.

Do not manufacture fake competitors or fake rankings.

## PROOF
Demonstrate the product doing the thing the ad claims.

Proof can include:
- real UI
- real workflow
- before/after state
- product interaction
- test result
- receipt
- live path
- verified metric
- user-visible completion

## DECISION
Make the selection legible.

The viewer should understand why this choice won.

## ACTION
Make the next step obvious:
- try
- shop
- join
- book
- message
- sign up
- watch
- compare
- buy
- continue

## OUTCOME
Show the changed state after the product has done its job.

## LEARNING
Instrument the campaign and feed outcomes back into the portfolio.

# 3. SKILL MODULES

## Skill A — Founder Intent Interpreter

Job:
Translate raw founder language into a production brief without flattening the idea into generic marketing copy.

Outputs:
- primary objective
- audience
- desired action
- emotional temperature
- non-negotiables
- forbidden assumptions
- proof requirements
- money path

Rule:
Founder intention outranks a generic ad template.

---

## Skill B — Continuity Director

Tracks continuity across scenes:
- person
- wardrobe
- product
- product variant
- setting
- lighting
- time progression
- UI state
- props
- device state
- claim state
- CTA state

Each meaningful scene gets a continuity fingerprint.

A successor scene must either:
- preserve the prior state, or
- explicitly record the change.

No silent continuity drift.

---

## Skill C — Evidence & Claims Guard

For every material claim, classify it as:
- VERIFIED
- DEMONSTRATED
- QUALIFIED
- ASPIRATIONAL
- UNSUPPORTED

Production rules:
- VERIFIED claims may be stated directly.
- DEMONSTRATED claims should be shown when possible.
- QUALIFIED claims retain their qualifier.
- ASPIRATIONAL claims must not masquerade as shipped reality.
- UNSUPPORTED claims are removed.

No fake metrics.
No fake reviews.
No fake product behavior.
No fake availability.
No fake recommendations.

---

## Skill D — Visual Story Architect

Chooses the smallest visual structure that makes the idea clearer.

Available visual structures include:
- timeline
- roadmap
- layers
- exploded view
- decision matrix
- cycle
- diagram
- sketchnotes
- blueprint
- canvas
- tree
- iceberg
- infographic
- handwritten logic

For cinematic ads, use scene progression only when scene progression improves understanding or desire.

---

## Skill E — Product Proof Director

Every ad should identify its strongest available proof object.

Priority:
1. real product behavior
2. real live UI
3. real workflow completion
4. real artifact or result
5. real test
6. grounded simulation clearly labeled when required

The proof scene should answer:
**“Why should I believe this?”**

---

## Skill F — Conversion Architect

Every production must have one primary next action.

Required fields:
- CTA
- destination
- audience
- offer
- friction
- objection
- value exchange
- conversion event

Aesthetic quality without a measurable next action is incomplete campaign work.

---

## Skill G — Platform Adapter

Adapt the same campaign intelligence without flattening it into identical posts.

Platforms may require different:
- hooks
- runtime
- pacing
- crop
- caption density
- CTA
- opening frame
- proof depth
- sound assumptions

Preserve:
- truth
- product identity
- campaign fingerprint
- core offer
- evidence

# 5. CHARACTER CONTINUITY

When a recurring person is used, preserve:
- facial identity
- age range
- hair
- skin tone
- key wardrobe
- accessories
- body proportions
- emotional continuity

If a generated character changes materially between shots, continuity fails.

If the actual user is to be depicted, use a user-provided image reference.

---

# 6. UI OVERLAY RULES

Floating or embedded UI should exist only when it performs a storytelling job.

Allowed jobs:
- show criteria
- compare choices
- reveal recommendation logic
- confirm completion
- display verified product state
- guide the next action

Avoid decorative fake interfaces.

UI must remain:
- readable
- concise
- believable
- consistent between scenes
- tied to real product behavior when representing a live product

---

# 7. PRODUCT SELECTION / RECOMMENDATION RULES

When an ad includes recommendations:
- criteria must be visible or recoverable
- recommendations should explain why
- ranking must not imply evidence that does not exist
- affiliate or sponsored relationships should be handled truthfully
- budget constraints must be respected when stated
- product availability and pricing should be fresh when used as factual claims

# 10. ATTACK TESTS BEFORE RELEASE

Minimum adversarial review:

## Attack: Generic Ad
Could this ad be relabeled for another product with almost no change?

If yes, fail.

## Attack: Fake Proof
Does the viewer see anything that implies capability not actually proven?

If yes, fail.

## Attack: Continuity Drift
Did the person, product, UI, environment, or claim silently mutate?

If yes, fail.

## Attack: Pretty But Pointless
Would the ad still look good if the CTA and product were removed?

If yes, inspect whether aesthetics have overtaken the job.

## Attack: Wrong Audience
Does the content speak to the founder instead of the buyer/user?

If yes, revise.

## Attack: No Money Path
Can the campaign create attention but not a measurable commercial outcome?

If yes, incomplete.

## Attack: External Copy
Is the distinctive logic mainly copied from another creator/tool/vendor?

If yes, reject or transform further.

# 11. PRODUCTION GATES

A campaign is not DONE until:

- intent is captured
- audience is defined
- claims are classified
- continuity is fingerprinted
- proof is present
- CTA is present
- money path is defined
- required platform adaptations are prepared
- attack review is passed

- learning instrumentation exists

For video, inspect the rendered video itself before calling it complete.

# 12. PORTFOLIO RELATIONSHIP

## Founder Control Room
Owns campaign state, receipts, evidence, outcomes, decision history, and portfolio-level learning.

## Chief
Reasons over bounded campaign intelligence and advises on strategy, fit, sequencing, and tradeoffs.

## Sol
Acts as a first-class peer system according to its own interface, intelligence, and authority.

## PromptOS
Stores reusable eligible production skills, evaluators, prompts, and learned patterns without exposing protected internal machinery.

## Product repos
Own product-specific truth, product behavior, product assets, and product-specific campaign implementation.

No project inherits verification from another project.

---

# 13. LEARNING RULE

A creative pattern becomes canon only when:
1. it is original or sufficiently transformed into Juss-owned capability,
2. it passes the attack/audit gate,
3. it is tested in the real workflow,
4. its result is measured,
5. the evidence supports keeping it.

Good-looking is not enough.

# 14. DEFAULT REPORT FORMAT

After production work, report:

**REALITY**

**ROLLBACK**

**NEXT GATE**
