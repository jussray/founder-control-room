# /sales

## Purpose

Create the strongest truthful path from a verified need to a mutually useful exchange. Optimize for durable trust, evidence, margin quality, retention, and founder control - not pressure, vanity activity, or fabricated certainty.

## Trigger

Use for positioning, offers, pricing analysis, qualification, outreach planning, conversion, retention, partnerships, economic opportunities, revenue operations, productization, product packaging, product launch, and commercialization.

For any productization or commercialization task, also read and apply [`docs/COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_GRADUATION_RULE.md`](../../docs/COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_GRADUATION_RULE.md).

## Required sequence

1. Complete 5W1H: buyer, decision owner, offer, channel, timing, reason, and authorized execution path.
2. Inspect current product, catalog, pricing authority, proof, customer boundary, funnel evidence, fulfillment, and constraints.
3. Qualify the real problem, fit, authority, budget or resource constraint, urgency, and disqualifiers.
4. Connect verified capabilities to an outcome using concrete proof and explicit limits.
5. Define scope, value exchange, fulfillment, support, risk reversal, and success criteria.
6. Answer objections only with evidence; record unknowns instead of inventing reassurance.
7. Propose the smallest reversible next commitment. No approval carries forward.
8. Define onboarding, delivery evidence, feedback, renewal, expansion, and exit conditions.
9. Measure qualified opportunities, conversion, margin quality, cycle time, retention, refunds, complaints, and evidence freshness.

## Outreach and negotiation response layer

Whenever this skill drafts or revises an external outreach response, partnership reply, vendor message, investor/funder response, customer sales reply, landlord/property message, hiring reply, or other consequential business conversation, silently apply:

```text
DISCOVER -> GOAL -> AUTHORITY -> BATNA -> VALUE -> EVIDENCE -> STRUCTURE -> TERMS -> RECEIPT
```

Interpret the sequence this way:

- **DISCOVER:** learn the other side's actual objective, constraint, success condition, decision process, and unresolved question before pitching. If material information is missing, ask one useful question rather than manufacturing certainty.
- **GOAL:** define the smallest truthful outcome the current exchange should advance.
- **AUTHORITY:** distinguish the person replying from the person who can approve, veto, fund, sign, buy, hire, rent, partner, or otherwise decide. Keep authority `UNKNOWN` until proven.
- **BATNA:** preserve a real alternative and founder control. Never invent competing offers, urgency, leverage, demand, or deadlines.
- **VALUE:** identify what Juss can credibly contribute that matters to this specific counterpart.
- **EVIDENCE:** support leverage with current proof, not adjectives, confidence, model output, or aspirational traction.
- **STRUCTURE:** when the current deal shape is weak, redesign scope, pilot size, milestones, sequence, payment timing, licensing, revenue share, proof exchange, or another reversible structure instead of forcing the original frame.
- **TERMS:** negotiate numbers and commitments only after the value, authority, constraints, and structure are sufficiently understood.
- **RECEIPT:** end with an observable next step, question, decision, or commitment that can be recorded and verified.

Response rules:

- Sequence information intentionally. Do not volunteer internal budget ceilings, desperation, maximum concessions, private fallback options, private strategy, or full negotiating limits merely because they are known.
- Never lie, hide a fact that must be disclosed, create a false impression, bluff a nonexistent alternative, or manufacture scarcity.
- Use leverage, not pressure. Valid leverage comes from credible alternatives, verified differentiated value, timing, evidence, and a well-designed structure.
- Regulate tone and use observable signals only. Do not pretend to know another person's hidden emotions, motives, or private constraints.
- Prefer a question-first response when discovery or authority is incomplete. Prefer a smaller reversible commitment when the counterparty is not ready for the full ask.
- Answer the actual inbound message first. Do not turn every reply into a pitch.
- Keep drafts human, direct, and channel-appropriate. The framework is backstage reasoning, not text to dump into the message.
- Preserve all existing privacy, consent, truth, founder-approval, pricing, publication, and project-boundary gates.

## Commercial product graduation

Use this state sequence for a sellable product or child-product candidate:

```text
INSPIRED
-> TRANSFORMED
-> ORIGINAL
-> USEFUL
-> VERIFIED
-> SELL
```

- Inspiration may trigger a product. It may never become the product.
- A polished marketing image, example page, workflow name, internal capability, or adjacent analytics signal does not by itself establish a sellable product.
- An umbrella product may reveal child products, but each child must independently prove customer value, deliverable substance, truthful promise, evidence, and delivery before graduation.
- Marketing can be cinematic and emotionally compelling, but factual claims may not exceed the current verified product substance.
- Native analytics can support observed account/time-window claims. They do not automatically prove product performance or causality.

## Product fingerprint + proof cookie

For material commercial work, maintain two non-secret continuity markers:

**Product fingerprint**

Bind the current product identity to its problem, promise, actual contents, source framework, version, commercial state, price/channel, authoritative evidence, and delivery path.

**Proof cookie**

Bind one exact product fingerprint to the release proof currently observed: contents inspected, claims checked, marketing checked against product truth, price/status read back, purchase path, delivery path, required customer path, evidence source, and observed time.

These markers:

- never grant authority;
- never become browser/device tracking identifiers;
- never carry approval forward;
- never prove an unobserved customer outcome; and
- expire for current claims when bound contents, price, listing state, checkout, delivery, runtime, provider, authority, or evidence materially changes.

Preserve expired fingerprints/cookies as historical provenance and reacquire proof before restoring current launch or performance claims.

## Operating workflow

Commercial work inherits the founder challenge stack rather than creating a separate operating system:

```text
Founder Intent
-> Confess / Reality
-> Authoritative Source
-> VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED
-> ULTRATHINK
-> Red Team 1 - premise
-> Lindy mode
-> L99
-> OODA
-> Bill Gates pass
-> Elon Musk pass
-> Red Team 2 - implementation
-> Smallest reversible build
-> Verification
-> Product fingerprint
-> Proof cookie
-> Graduation state
-> Rollback / Next Gate
```

The Bill Gates and Elon Musk labels are internal heuristic passes, not endorsements, partnerships, or claims of affiliation.

## Boundaries

- Never invent demand, scarcity, testimonials, savings, eligibility, performance, inventory, delivery timing, or authority.
- Never use dark patterns, deceptive urgency, hidden fees, bait-and-switch, or sensitive personal information for persuasion.
- Do not use raw teen, journal, voice, media, health, parent-visibility, customer-secret, or private founder data in sales analysis or outreach.
- Do not send messages, publish offers, change prices, approve discounts, spend funds, submit applications, contact external parties, or execute checkout without the exact founder gate.
- Keep provider, project, catalog, customer, vendor, credential, checkout, and fulfillment boundaries intact.
- Workflow names, challenge passes, fingerprints, and proof cookies never widen publication, checkout, provider, pricing, merge, deployment, spending, or customer-contact authority.

## Output

Return buyer and owner, verified problem and disqualifiers, offer and non-goals, proof used and missing, objections, next commitment, economics assumptions, risks, rollback, exact provenance, product fingerprint, proof cookie status, commercial graduation state, and next approval gate.

A sales plan is not authorization.
