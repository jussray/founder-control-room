# /sales

## Purpose

Create the strongest truthful path from a verified need to a mutually useful exchange. Optimize for durable trust, evidence, margin quality, retention, and founder control, not pressure, vanity activity, or fabricated certainty.

## Trigger

Use for positioning, offers, pricing analysis, qualification, outreach planning, conversion, retention, partnerships, economic opportunities, and revenue operations.

## Required sequence

1. Complete 5W1H: buyer, decision owner, offer, channel, timing, reason, and authorized execution path.
2. Inspect current product, catalog, pricing authority, proof, customer boundary, funnel evidence, fulfillment, and constraints.
3. Qualify the real problem, fit, authority, budget or resource constraint, urgency, and disqualifiers.
4. Connect verified capabilities to an outcome using concrete proof and explicit limits.
5. Define scope, value exchange, fulfillment, support, risk reversal, and success criteria.
6. Answer objections only with evidence; record unknowns instead of inventing reassurance.
7. Propose the smallest reversible next commitment. No approval carries forward.
8. Define onboarding, delivery evidence, feedback, renewal, expansion, and exit conditions.
9. Measure qualified opportunities, conversion, margin quality, cycle time, retention, refunds, complaints, and evidence freshness.

## Seven-day revenue strike mode

Activate this mode when the founder sets an aggressive short-horizon revenue target such as a seven-day sprint.

Preserve the ambition, but correct the measurement before optimizing the plan. Track these independently:

```text
cash_collected
signed_contract_value
qualified_pipeline
speculative_opportunity
```

Never merge those planes into one headline number. Borrowing is not revenue. A grant application is not revenue. An unsigned proposal is not a contract. An invoice is not cash. A verbal yes is not a signed commitment. A click, view, reply, booked call, or CRM stage is not customer value.

The default strike objective is:

```text
maximize legitimate cash collected
+ maximize qualified signed contract value
+ preserve delivery capacity, trust, margin quality, and founder control
```

A numerical stretch target may guide prioritization. It may never be reported as achieved without authoritative receipts.

### Live revenue reality

Before choosing an offer or target list, inspect the narrowest available authoritative evidence across relevant connected surfaces. Use all relevant connected skills and plugins, but do not call a tool merely because it exists.

Typical evidence order:

1. provider-native cash/payment receipt for actual money received;
2. signed contract, accepted quote, or equivalent binding commercial evidence;
3. qualified buyer conversation with decision authority, problem, timing, and economic fit;
4. CRM deal/contact/company state with freshness and provenance;
5. Gmail or other consent-valid communication showing a real current need or request;
6. verified public professional/company information useful for qualification;
7. repository/runtime proof showing the capability being sold actually exists;
8. inference or market hypothesis, explicitly marked as such.

Record missing source coverage as `UNKNOWN`; do not translate an unscanned inbox, disconnected plugin, absent CRM field, or unavailable provider into `nothing exists`.

### Revenue truth state machine

Use this progression and require new evidence for every promotion:

```text
IDENTIFIED
-> CONTACTABLE
-> ENGAGED
-> QUALIFIED
-> PROPOSAL_READY
-> PROPOSAL_SENT
-> NEGOTIATING
-> SIGNED
-> INVOICED
-> CASH_COLLECTED
-> FULFILLED
-> CUSTOMER_VALUE_VERIFIED
```

No state authorizes the next. A record may move backward when evidence becomes stale, contradictory, revoked, disputed, unpaid, refunded, or superseded.

Count `qualified_pipeline` only when the buyer problem, decision owner or path, timing, fit, and plausible economic scope are evidenced. Count `signed_contract_value` only from binding signed/accepted evidence. Count `cash_collected` only from provider-native or equivalent authoritative receipt. Deduplicate one commercial opportunity across Gmail, HubSpot, LinkedIn, Stripe, Shopify, proposals, and internal records before aggregation.

### Lindy screen

Prefer the shortest path that also makes sense one year from now:

- sell a verified outcome already supported by existing products, proof, or expertise before inventing a new product;
- prefer reusable audits, implementation sprints, licenses, support, retainers, and bounded enterprise services over one-off tricks;
- keep the offer understandable without hype, hidden implementation detail, or founder overwork;
- preserve source/IP boundaries while exposing enough proof to earn trust;
- do not create product work during a revenue sprint unless a qualified buyer makes that work necessary to close or fulfill the sale;
- reject tactics that require deception, spam, artificial scarcity, financial pressure, or permanent reputation debt.

### OODA revenue loop

**Observe:** reacquire current buyer, pipeline, proof, provider, and reply state. Separate verified, inferred, unknown, stale, and blocked.

**Orient:** rank opportunities by verified pain, decision access, urgency, economic value, proof fit, delivery capacity, cycle time, and trust risk. Protect project boundaries and sensitive data.

**Decide:** choose one highest-leverage reversible commercial action. Define evidence, kill condition, rollback, and exact founder approval gate before external effect.

**Act:** prepare or execute only what current authority permits. After an authorized external action, read the destination/provider state independently and update the commercial truth plane.

Loop when the buyer replies, provider state changes, proof changes, the offer changes, a deadline passes, or a prior assumption is contradicted.

## Attack-1000 commercial red team

For a material revenue strike, run exactly 1,000 adversarial probes as the Cartesian product:

```text
10 truth domains x 10 failure modes x 10 adversary lenses = 1,000 probes
```

### 10 truth domains

1. buyer/problem;
2. demand/timing;
3. offer/scope;
4. pricing/economics;
5. proof/claims;
6. authority/consent/legal boundary;
7. channel/deliverability;
8. fulfillment/capacity;
9. contract/payment/collection;
10. CRM/attribution/freshness.

### 10 failure modes

1. stale state;
2. wrong identity or wrong authority;
3. missing evidence;
4. incentive or metric gaming;
5. duplicate/double-counted outcome;
6. partial failure;
7. provider/channel outage or policy drift;
8. unauthorized action or scope expansion;
9. irreversible lock-in or failed rollback;
10. adversarial misuse, fraud, dispute, or reputational harm.

### 10 adversary lenses

1. buyer;
2. procurement/legal;
3. finance;
4. security/privacy;
5. operator/fulfillment team;
6. competitor;
7. channel/provider;
8. FutureYou / maintenance;
9. failure/recovery operator;
10. public/reputation reviewer.

Evaluate every combination. Emit only material non-pass findings to preserve token budget. Each non-pass finding must carry one of `CONTROL`, `HOLD`, or `KILL`, plus the smallest evidence or control needed to clear it. A superficial pass over ten categories is not an Attack-1000.

### Redteam I - premise attack

Before selecting the offer, attempt to disprove the revenue thesis:

- Is there a real current buyer problem, or only founder enthusiasm?
- Is the buyer able and likely to pay within the stated horizon?
- Does the founder have defensible proof of the promised capability?
- Is the proposed price connected to buyer economics rather than the revenue target?
- Is an existing warm path stronger than cold acquisition?
- Would a smaller offer close faster without damaging the longer-term opportunity?
- Is the plan secretly dependent on financing, grants, speculation, virality, spam, or an unverified whale?
- Would fulfillment consume more founder capacity than the contract is worth?

If the premise fails, revise or kill it before outreach.

### Redteam II - selected-plan attack

After one offer and channel path are selected, attack the exact implementation:

- Can the same lead be counted through multiple systems?
- Can a reply be mistaken for qualification?
- Can a signed contract be mistaken for collected cash?
- Can cash be mistaken for fulfilled customer value?
- Can a stale CRM stage outrank a newer buyer message?
- Can an automation send without exact founder approval or valid consent?
- Can private source, teen/family data, credentials, or proprietary logic leak into sales proof?
- Can a pricing exception silently become a standing discount?
- Can provider success be mistaken for destination receipt?
- Can the sprint create commitments the current team cannot fulfill?
- Can a buyer dispute, refund, chargeback, cancellation, or failed delivery reveal that the apparent win was not durable?

Do not promote the plan while a material `HOLD` or `KILL` finding remains unresolved.

- **Juss Founder OS / Founder AI Operator Workflow:** intent, authority, freshness, evidence, rollback, fingerprints, continuity.
- **/sales:** buyer, problem, offer, economics, qualification, conversion, retention.
- **/devil:** Redteam I and Redteam II.
- **Unified Growth Inbox:** consent, identity, channel windows, draft-only default, delivery evidence, opt-out and human escalation.
- **GitHub:** exact repository/product proof and current source evidence. Source green is not customer outcome.
- **Gmail:** warm-relation evidence, buyer replies, requests, and draft preparation. Do not infer unseen conversations and do not send without the current approval gate.
- **HubSpot:** CRM opportunity/contact/company state, stage, activity, amount, association, and dedup evidence. CRM stage is not cash.
- **LinkedIn:** public professional discovery and qualification only when the connected capability supports it. Do not claim inbox or message access from profile search.
- **Stripe / Shopify / other payment provider:** payment configuration and provider-native payment evidence when applicable. Do not create charges, payment links, products, discounts, subscriptions, refunds, or price mutations without exact authority.
- **Google Drive / Docs / Sheets / Slides:** approved proposals, proof packets, pricing/source documents, and durable operating records when they are the authoritative carrier.
- **Automation / n8n / Zapier:** observation, normalization, drafts, and founder-approved bounded sequences only. A run receipt proves execution, not sale or customer value.

Record tool coverage and failure explicitly. A plugin result can supply evidence. It cannot manufacture demand, consent, authority, revenue, or customer value.

## Revenue fingerprints and continuity cookie

Revenue-strike continuity must reuse `juss-v10/operator-continuity@v2` and `operatorContinuityDimensionFingerprint` from `src/lib/operatorContinuity.ts` rather than creating a second continuity authority.

At each meaningful OODA cycle, compute deterministic SHA-256 dimension fingerprints over canonicalized, minimized state:

```text
offerFingerprint
  = offer id/version + buyer segment + scope + non-goals + pricing assumptions + success criteria

pipelineFingerprint
  = deduplicated opportunity ids + truth states + bounded amounts/currencies + decision-path state + evidence freshness

proofFingerprint
  = exact product/repository/provider proof identities used to support the offer

toolCoverageFingerprint
  = relevant connected-source coverage + last authoritative observation + explicit missing coverage

authorityFingerprint
  = current founder gates for outreach + price/discount + contract + payment + publication + delivery
```

Bind them into the existing operator continuity receipt as appropriate:

```text
scopeFingerprint    <- offerFingerprint
proofFingerprint    <- proofFingerprint + pipelineFingerprint
providerFingerprint <- toolCoverageFingerprint
authorityFingerprint <- authorityFingerprint
runtimeFingerprint  <- exact runtime proof fingerprint only when runtime behavior is part of the commercial claim; otherwise null
```

The revenue continuity cookie is an execution/continuation receipt, never an HTTP browser cookie and never approval. It must preserve the existing invariants:

```text
browserCookie: false
authorizing: false
standingMergeAuthority: false
approvalCarryForward: false
founderDecisionRequired: true
```

During a seven-day strike, expire revenue-state continuity no later than 24 hours after observation, and sooner when a buyer reply, deal state, price, proof subject, provider state, contract state, payment state, or authority state changes. A predecessor fingerprint is provenance only. It cannot renew stale evidence.

Never store message bodies, private customer content, raw emails, phone numbers, payment credentials, secrets, teen/family content, chain-of-thought, or other unnecessary personal data inside a fingerprint/cookie payload. Store only bounded identifiers, classifications, hashes, timestamps, and evidence references needed for reacquisition.

## Revenue strike stop / kill conditions

Stop or downgrade the plan when any of these is true:

- no current evidence of a material buyer problem;
- buyer identity, consent, or decision authority is ambiguous;
- the economics require deception, fabricated scarcity, predatory pressure, or an unsupported performance claim;
- the plan counts borrowing, investment, grants, speculative asset gains, unpaid invoices, unsigned proposals, or duplicate CRM entries as revenue;
- fulfillment cannot be delivered safely within the promised scope;
- proof depends on sensitive/private data that cannot be disclosed;
- the requested external action exceeds current founder/provider authority;
- payment, contract, delivery, refund, or dispute state is contradictory or stale;
- the 1,000-probe attack leaves a material `KILL` or unresolved `HOLD`.

## Boundaries

- Never invent demand, scarcity, testimonials, savings, eligibility, performance, inventory, delivery timing, or authority.
- Never use dark patterns, deceptive urgency, hidden fees, bait-and-switch, or sensitive personal information for persuasion.
- Do not use raw teen, journal, voice, media, health, parent-visibility, customer-secret, or private founder data in sales analysis or outreach.
- Do not send messages, publish offers, change prices, approve discounts, spend funds, submit applications, contact external parties, or execute checkout without the exact founder gate.
- Keep provider, project, catalog, customer, vendor, credential, checkout, and fulfillment boundaries intact.
