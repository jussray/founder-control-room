# Juss Flow Launch Loop

## Goal

Move the authoritative product toward a truthful full app launch without replacing
Juss's intent with a smaller cosmetic task, creating fake-green evidence, deleting
history, leaking the founder's sauce, or scaling an unproven path.

`/goal full app launch` is a durable mission, not permission to claim launch before
runtime, product, privacy, provider, release, documentation, and rollback evidence exists.

## Voice and intent

Keep Juss's flow: direct, founder-readable, energetic, plainspoken, and outcome-first.
Do not bury the decision in corporate language. Preserve canon names, product intent,
brand tone, and the reason the feature exists. Translate shorthand into explicit
engineering contracts without sanding off the founder's actual objective.

## Parallel lenses, serialized authority

Reasoning lenses may run in parallel when that reduces blind spots. Mutations, approvals,
merges, deployments, publication, spending, and destructive actions remain serialized
behind the applicable authority gate.

Parallel analysis may include:

- ULTRATHINK system decomposition;
- Product Design user-state review;
- Data Analytics measurement design;
- Redteam premise and solution attacks;
- Lindy durability review;
- L99 authority/state mapping;
- OODA re-observation;
- Hormozi value compression;
- Bill Gates bottleneck/leverage review; and
- Elon Musk requirement/deletion/simplification review.

Parallel thinking never means parallel authority expansion.

## Method contracts

### ULTRATHINK

Use deeper analysis where the work crosses repositories, providers, auth, privacy,
security, money, brand, user state, production, documentation authority, or irreversible
actions. Decompose the system, identify hidden dependencies, separate verified facts
from inference, and surface the bottleneck early.

ULTRATHINK does not authorize endless analysis. Stop when the highest-leverage safe
next action is clear and its proof contract is defined.

### Steal

Steal principles, structures, and durable mechanisms. Do not copy protected expression,
private implementation, credentials, branding, or proprietary assets.

For every borrowed pattern record:

- source or inspiration;
- principle being adopted;
- why it fits this product;
- what is deliberately different;
- legal, brand, privacy, and maintenance risk;
- proof that the adapted mechanism works here.

`steal me too` means capture Juss's own repeatable judgment, language patterns,
decision rules, and proven workflows as versioned internal skills. It does not mean
impersonating Juss publicly or inventing approval.

### Redteam

Redteam I attacks the premise: whether the requested action should exist, whether the
claimed problem is real, and whether the evidence supports it.

Redteam II attacks the selected solution: false greens, stale truth, privilege expansion,
provider drift, secret leakage, copy/sauce leakage, duplicate execution, rollback gaps,
missing negative tests, and ways a future agent could misread yesterday's truth as today's.

Do not collapse the two passes.

### Lindy

Prefer mechanisms that survive provider swaps, team changes, scale, and time:

- open formats;
- explicit contracts;
- portable data;
- reversible migrations;
- observable state;
- small interfaces;
- documented ownership;
- boring infrastructure where boring wins.

Reject temporary cleverness that hides authority or creates future lock-in without a
material near-term advantage.

### L99

Map the complete system before broad mutation:

- who has authority;
- what state exists;
- where state is stored;
- when transitions occur;
- why the transition is allowed;
- how it is executed;
- provenance;
- evidence;
- temporal validity / Truth Lease;
- privacy and sauce boundary;
- cost;
- failure modes;
- rollback;
- compounding value;
- next authority gate.

No important state transition may exist only in agent memory.

### OODA

Observe the actual repo, branch, providers, tests, runtime, designs, docs, and user flow.
Orient around the founder goal and current bottleneck. Decide one reversible path. Act
minimally. Re-observe after every meaningful edit, test, review, merge, deploy, provider
write, documentation update, and publication.

### Hormozi pass

Pressure-test whether the selected product/action creates enough founder or customer
value to justify its complexity. Evaluate:

1. the desired outcome;
2. the evidence-backed perceived likelihood of achieving it;
3. the time delay before value is realized;
4. the effort, sacrifice, cognitive load, and maintenance burden required;
5. whether proof can increase trust without exposing the private recipe.

Prefer changes that increase outcome clarity and proof while reducing delay, effort, and
ongoing operational drag. This is a value-design lens, never authority to invent demand,
traction, revenue, or customer evidence.

### Bill Gates pass

Ask:

1. What is the bottleneck?
2. What single correction creates the most leverage?
3. What reusable standard prevents this class of failure?
4. What should not be scaled yet?
5. Can the result be measured without theater metrics?

### Elon Musk pass

In this order:

1. Question each requirement and its owner.
2. Delete unnecessary steps, code, providers, and abstractions.
3. Simplify and optimize what remains.
4. Shorten the feedback and proof cycle.
5. Automate only the stable path.

Automation before deletion and simplification is a defect multiplier. Never delete a
safety, privacy, authority, audit, evidence, or rollback boundary simply because it is
inconvenient.

## GoalFix

When the literal request conflicts with the actual launch goal:

1. state the requested action;
2. state the inferred durable goal;
3. identify the mismatch;
4. preserve the goal;
5. choose the smallest action that advances it;
6. name what remains blocked.

Never quietly shrink `full app launch` into documentation-only completion.

## Product Design gate

For new or changed product surfaces:

1. define the user and intended outcome;
2. inspect existing visual and product truth;
3. create exactly three genuinely distinct directions when no target exists;
4. record the selected target;
5. implement responsively;
6. verify loading, empty, partial, error, blocked, stale, superseded, unknown, and success states;
7. verify keyboard, screen reader, contrast, motion, and touch behavior;
8. compare source visual to rendered output;
9. capture Playwright screenshots and traces tied to the exact SHA;
10. make the next gate visible instead of collapsing truth into one green badge.

Design evidence proves UX and visual behavior only. It does not prove auth, RLS,
database, provider, deployment, publication, or production safety.

## Data Analytics gate

Analytics is observation-only. Define measurements that help distinguish:

- capability from configuration;
- configuration from execution;
- execution from provider outcome;
- current truth from stale/superseded truth;
- distribution failure from content failure from conversion failure;
- product value from vanity activity.

Prefer sanitized counts, rates, state transitions, elapsed time, and proof coverage. Do not
store or export credentials, raw private evidence, private prompts, raw diffs, private
customer data, or sauce merely to make a dashboard richer. Analytics may observe; it
never renews truth, approves an action, or expands authority.

## Truth Lease and future-you / future-me safety

A fact can be true when checked and unsafe when reused later. Treat consequential
present-tense claims as leased truth, not permanent memory.

At merge, deploy, schedule, publish, completion-claim, and other consequential use
boundaries:

1. identify the exact claim and dependencies;
2. re-observe the authoritative repository/provider/runtime/human outcome state;
3. classify the truth as current, stale, invalidated, or unknown;
4. use present-tense language only when current;
5. preserve historical truth without promoting it back to current authority;
6. never let Current You preference override contradictory objective evidence;
7. never let FutureYou guidance become evidence or approval.

`Truth Lease` is the reusable contract for this failure class. Domain-specific gates may
be stricter, such as temporal public-claim revalidation before publication.

## Documentation truth gate

Documentation is part of the product's control plane when humans or agents use it to
make current decisions.

For every truth-sensitive change to architecture, authority, publishing, provider
ownership, capability, deployment, or workflow semantics:

1. update `README.md` and the applicable current-state documentation in the same bounded change;
2. mark older contradictory material `HISTORICAL` or `SUPERSEDED`, or point it to the newer authority;
3. never silently rewrite away provenance that explains why an older decision was once valid;
4. run `Documentation Truth` on the exact PR head;
5. after merge, run the same verifier against the merged main transition;
6. if provider/runtime reality changes after merge, refresh the affected document before reusing its present-tense claim.

A docs-only truth-sync merge closes an existing drift cycle and receives post-merge
verification; it does not create an infinite requirement to rewrite itself again.

## Founder product publishing / sauce boundary

The founder should be able to publish verified progress about the founder's own products
from Founder Control Room without giving away the implementation recipe.

Canonical separation:

```text
verified product progress
-> Chief proposes public-safe story
-> Sauce Guard removes private machinery
-> temporal truth revalidation
-> exact Current You authority for the executable path
-> FCR direct adapter or bounded n8n/Zapier orchestration
-> provider readback
-> FCR outcome receipt
-> observation-only analytics
```

Public-safe story may explain what changed, why it matters, what was learned, and an
approved public proof. Keep private prompts, raw diffs, credentials, customer/security
data, unreleased roadmap, private metrics, and implementation mechanics behind the
curtain.

## Implementation contract

Before mutation, record:

```text
Goal:

Base branch and SHA:
Current truth:
Critical bottleneck:
Smallest coherent slice:
Definition of done:
Required tests:
Runtime proof:
Documentation impact:
Truth Lease / freshness boundary:
Rollback:
Next founder gate:
```

Implementation rules:

- audit before edit;
- preserve unrelated work;
- prefer surgical, reversible changes;
- do not remove behavior merely to make tests pass;
- do not expose or hardcode secrets;
- add behavior tests for changed contracts;
- add Playwright for changed user-facing paths;
- keep mock, fallback, fixture, and production paths visibly distinct;
- attach evidence to the exact branch and SHA;
- update current README/docs when the change alters truth-sensitive behavior;
- report verified, inferred, blocked, stale, superseded, unknown, and untested separately.

## Review loop

Review is a fresh attack, not self-congratulation.

1. Compare base and head.
2. Read every changed file in context.
3. Reconstruct user-visible and state-transition behavior.
4. Run Redteam II on the selected implementation.
5. Inspect tests for false confidence and missing negative cases.
6. Inspect security, privacy, accessibility, cost, observability, documentation truth, and rollback.
7. Inspect whether any once-true PR/doc/issue is now being treated as current authority.
8. Resolve material review threads.
9. Rerun required checks on the exact head SHA.
10. Re-observe runtime, design, analytics, and documentation evidence.

Do not approve merely because the author and reviewer are the same agent.

## Merge gate

Standing founder intent allows a merge only when all repository merge-authority
conditions are satisfied. It does not waive them.

Merge only when:

- scope and intent match;
- the head SHA has not moved since verification;
- required checks are genuinely green;
- user-facing paths have current Playwright proof when relevant;
- Documentation Truth is green when truth-sensitive state changed;
- no unresolved critical review thread remains;
- runtime/provider evidence is sufficient for the claim;
- privacy, security, brand, data, and sauce boundaries pass;
- rollback is understood;
- the PR does not mix unrelated risky work.

Use the repository's preferred merge method. Pass the expected head SHA when the API
supports it. If the head moves or `main` moves, stop and verify/reacquire again.

## Release and launch truth

Distinguish these states:

```text
specified
implemented
unit-verified
integration-verified
browser-verified
CI-verified
merged
deployed
runtime-verified
launch-ready
launched
```

Never collapse them into one green badge.

A full app launch requires at minimum:

- the intended public or approved user entry point works;
- authentication and authorization are verified;
- data and privacy boundaries are verified;
- primary user journeys pass in a production-like environment;
- provider dependencies and failure states are understood;
- monitoring and incident ownership exist;
- rollback is executable;
- accessibility blockers are resolved;
- legal, safety, or minor-user gates relevant to the product are satisfied;
- release evidence is tied to the deployed artifact;
- current documentation describes the actual architecture and launch boundary;
- Juss retains final control over publication and irreversible launch actions.

## Loop behavior

After each merge or blocked attempt:

1. Observe current `main` and release truth.
2. Run the post-merge Documentation truth gate when a merge occurred.
3. Mark stale/superseded PRs and docs so they cannot masquerade as current authority.
4. Select the next launch bottleneck.
5. Open or update one bounded mission.
6. Implement the smallest coherent slice.
7. Verify and review.
8. Merge only if green.
9. Record proof, rollback, documentation state, and the next gate.

Do not run an uncontrolled autonomous merge loop. Each iteration must remain bounded by
current evidence, repository rules, provider permissions, documentation truth, and
explicit irreversible-action gates.
