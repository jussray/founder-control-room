# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately. The reporting address is **not yet published**: it is held until the mailbox is confirmed as configured and monitored. Include the affected project or domain, a concise description, steps to reproduce when safe, and the potential impact. Do not include passwords, private keys, sensitive personal information, or data from other users.

Do not open a public issue containing vulnerability details. We will review reports and coordinate appropriate next steps; no response-time or remediation-time guarantee is currently published.

## Scope and support

This policy applies to the Founder Control Room repository and its owned deployments. Supported versions and deployment coverage are evaluated case by case; no blanket version-support claim is made.

Testing must be authorized and must not disrupt services, access other users' information, or modify production data. This policy does not grant permission for intrusive testing.

## Disclosure endpoint

No `security.txt` endpoint is published by this repository. It will be added only after the reporting mailbox is verified.

## Maintenance

The reporting mailbox must be verified as configured and monitored before it is published. Until then, do not add a `security.txt` endpoint.
