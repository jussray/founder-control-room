Attack 2000 is a reasoning pressure budget, never a claim that 2,000 external tests ran. Project-local visual canon may specialize this law and may be stricter; it may not turn visual communication into generic, literal, manipulative, proofless, or inaccessible output.

That contract declares intended repository capability only.
