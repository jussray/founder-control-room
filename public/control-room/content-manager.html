Founder Control Room owns

Local authority and evidence

  • Exact source repository, commit, proof, and temporal claim meaning.
  • Public-safe draft boundaries and sauce withholding.
  • Fresh Current You approval, one-shot reservation, and first-party dispatch authority.
  • Provider readback capture, retained outcome receipt, rollback path, and next gate.

External platform owns

Terminal platform state

  • External account authorization and target account identity.
  • Terminal post ID, delivery state, platform errors, and ambiguity.
  • Published timestamps and externally observed post state.
  • Available platform engagement metrics.

Fail closed

A missing truth gate, provider configuration gap, rejected or ambiguous write, or absent readback is BLOCKED or UNKNOWN.

FCR must never translate capability, approval, dispatch, a provider request, or missing provider evidence into “published.” Publication requires terminal provider readback bound to the authorized execution.
