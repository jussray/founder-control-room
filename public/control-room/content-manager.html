First-party conveyor

Progress → story → sauce guard → FCR draft → approval → provider → learning

Proof and public disclosure are separate decisions. The system may know exactly why a claim is true without forcing that evidence link into every post.

  1. 1

    Verified progress

    Read exact project state and keep runtime, repository, and product claims scoped to what the evidence actually proves.

    internal evidence lineage
  2. 2

    Chief story

    Chief AI chooses the founder angle and proposes public-safe copy. It remains proposal-only.

    chief-ai/founder-content-proposal
  3. 3

    Sauce guard

    Strip raw diffs, secrets, private prompts, internal notes, and implementation detail that does not need to be public.

    sauce_leak_count = 0

    FCR draft

    FCR becomes the canonical content record. Public proof links are optional; internal proof is not.

    fcr/first-party-founder-content

    Current You approval

    Approval binds to the exact content hash and expires. Stale history and FutureYou cannot silently approve a changed post.

    edit → approval invalid

    Provider handoff

    LinkedIn direct, Buffer, or Cambiante receives only the approved public payload. Provider credentials and external state remain downstream.

    replaceable adapter
  7. 7

    Outcome learning

    Measure qualified conversations and attributed outcomes without storing raw post text, DMs, comments, or private provider payloads.

    content hash → outcomes

Chief AI owns

Story intelligence

  • Angle, hook, framing, and founder voice.
  • Public-safe claim proposal from verified progress.
  • Editorial suggestion for whether a public proof link helps the story.
  • No approval or provider-write authority.

Founder Control Room owns

Authority and learning

  • Canonical draft and private evidence lineage.
  • Sauce guard and exact-content approval hash.
  • Current-You approval, expiry, and handoff permission.
  • Privacy-safe conversion and qualified-conversation metrics.

Providers own

External delivery

  • OAuth/account identity and platform post IDs.
  • Actual scheduling/publication API state.
  • Provider retries, errors, and native engagement metrics.
  • Never the canonical copy or founder authority.

Truth boundary

First-party authority does not mean a provider write already happened.

FCR can prepare, protect, approve, and hand off the exact public payload. LinkedIn OAuth/API authorization remains a separate provider capability and must be read back before “published” is claimed.
