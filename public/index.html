The four jobs

The site is not a decorative dashboard. It is the working surface for seeing, deciding, acting, and verifying.

01

See portfolio reality

Open every registered project, inspect live repository state, browse files, connections, releases, and blockers.

02

Build through missions

Turn a founder goal into a scoped mission with a builder, reviewer, required checks, and a visible status lane.

03

Approve exact actions

Keep branch, patch, merge, terminal, deploy, and rollback authority separate instead of bundling risky permissions.

04

Verify proof and cost

Read activity, evidence, council input, checks, terminal output, and operating costs before calling anything done.
