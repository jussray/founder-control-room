Chief

Authority for agent work.

Chief turns founder intent into governed execution. It keeps permission, leases, receipts, evidence, and claims connected so an agent cannot quietly promote its own work into truth.

Intent→Authority→Lease→Receipt→Evidence
Why Chief exists

Execution is not outcome

A provider can accept an action while the human result is still unknown. Chief keeps that distinction explicit.

Proof Mode

Receipts say what executed. Evidence says what can be observed. Founder authority decides what may be claimed next.

PromptOS

Intent becomes a reusable operating asset.

PromptOS is an intention compiler. It takes what the founder means, preserves the intent, and turns it into prompts, commands, workflows, skills, constraints, tests, aliases, and stop conditions that can be reused instead of rewritten from scratch.

Intent→Constraints→Workflow→Verification→Reusable asset
Preserve meaning

Improve the script, not the founder away

PromptOS can make instructions clearer and more executable without changing the outcome the founder actually asked for.

Remember patterns

Approved workflows compound

Good operating patterns become named assets with authority, privacy, schemas, versioning, aliases, tests, and rollback behavior attached.

Generate new systems

Patterns become factories

Once a workflow is proven, PromptOS can use its structure to create the next prompt, skill, command, or operating procedure.
