The MOM8 challenge

Each day earns a square only when there is evidence. No pretending a plan is a result. No collapsing five blockers into one red light. One proof, one receipt.

01

Build

Ship one useful artifact, screen, workflow, or service improvement.

02

Fix

Repair one verified blocker with the smallest reversible change.

03

Prove

Capture real-path evidence: a test, screenshot, receipt, or measurable outcome.

04

Ask

Make one concrete offer, outreach message, or customer ask.

05

Learn

Turn a failure or surprise into one changed operating rule.

06

Earn

Record legitimate revenue only when money actually clears.

07

Teach

Explain one lesson simply enough that another builder can use it.

08

Compound

Choose the next move from evidence, not excitement.

Rules of the game

The challenge is designed to be shareable without turning into a financial promise.

1. Proof beats claims.

2. A blocker is allowed. Hiding it is not.

3. Never promise price, returns, ownership, or revenue share.

4. Do not use children’s names, faces, or private details.

5. Share the build, not a pump.
